# Quran recitation audio (Juz Amma) — provenance and licence

37 recordings, one per surah, covering Juz Amma (surahs 78 An-Naba
through 114 An-Nas) — the short surahs most commonly recited. Bundled
for offline playback (no network fetch, per the app's offline-first
rule), at the lowest bitrate the source offers (32kbps).

## Why only Juz Amma, not the whole Quran

The source collection's full 114-surah set, even at 32kbps, totals
~294MB — more than 3x this app's entire size at the time this was
added. This app has zero INTERNET permission, by design, so there is
no streaming fallback to fall back on for the rest: it's bundle
everything or bundle a deliberate subset. Juz Amma was chosen as the
subset because it's what's most commonly read/memorised first and in
daily prayer, at ~11MB total — a small, honest addition to the app's
size rather than a 3-4x one.

If a full-Quran bundle is ever wanted despite the size, or a better-
compressed/smaller-licensed source is found, prefer that and update
this file's provenance section to match.
