noor Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 August 2026

Summary

noor does not collect, store, or transmit personal data to us or any third party. There are no ads, analytics, accounts, or cloud sync.

Location

If enabled, device location is used on-device to calculate prayer times and Qibla direction. It is never transmitted. A district can be selected manually instead.

Local data

Prayer history, bookmarks, dhikr counts, Zakat entries, and settings remain in the device's encrypted local database. Uninstalling the app deletes this data.

Permissions

noor may request location, notifications, exact alarms, Do Not Disturb access for Silent Mode, and battery-optimization exemption for reliable reminders. noor requests no internet permission.

Contact

Questions can be sent through the Support noor screen in the app.
