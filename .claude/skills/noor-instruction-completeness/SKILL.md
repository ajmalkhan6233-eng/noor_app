# noor Instruction Completeness

## Rule
Before starting work on any message containing multiple distinct
requests: list every one out explicitly, numbered, in your own words,
before touching any code. Work through the full list. In the final
report, account for every single numbered item by name — done,
partially done, or blocked — never silently omit one because it was
harder or took longer than the others.

## Why this exists
The person has repeatedly experienced giving a list of several things
and having only one or two actually get addressed, with no
explanation for the rest. That's not acceptable, whether the cause was
running out of time, a hard technical problem, or losing track.

## In practice
- Numbered list first, from the actual instruction, not a summary.
- If something is going to take much longer than the others or is
  blocked, say so explicitly against its own number — don't just move
  on quietly.
- End every multi-part task with: "Here's every item from your list
  and its real status" — not a general paragraph.
